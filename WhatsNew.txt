- New "Calm" section with three quiet toys: no music, no stars, nothing to win.
- Busy Board: flip a light switch, push the big button, turn the color knob, play the xylophone, ring the doorbell to find Mochi, and slide the beads.
- Blocks: drag wooden blocks off the shelf and stack them. The wind button sweeps them away.
