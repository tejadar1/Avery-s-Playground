- Go Fishing: fish with a fishing rod. Drag the red-and-white bobber over a fish and let go while its mouth is open to catch it. Letting go over open water lets you try again.
- Spell has levels: three-letter words, then four letters like frog and milk, then longer words like pizza and zebra. Five spelled words open the next level, and stars add up at the top.
- Spell has "My words" (the heart): her name, Mom, Dad, Coco and Mochi.
- Spell has free spelling (the keyboard): each letter says its sound, and the speaker reads back what she wrote, even made-up words.
- Every letter now has a sound, including k, l, r, j, y, z and q, in Spell and ABC.
