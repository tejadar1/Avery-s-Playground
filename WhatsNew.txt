- Seven new science, building and coding games in Learn.
- Balance Scale: drag things onto the two pans and watch the heavy side go down, or balance when they weigh the same.
- Sink or Float: guess first, then drop it in the water and see. A wrong guess is a happy surprise.
- Coco Code: line up arrow cards and press Go to walk Coco to her cookie, around rocks on later levels. Early coding, like the floor robots in preschool.
- Big to Small: build a tower from the biggest block to the smallest, like the Montessori pink tower.
- Life Cycles: put the stages in order: seed to flower, egg to hen, eggs to tadpole to frog, egg to caterpillar to cocoon to butterfly.
- Ramps: pick a ramp and a ball, car or box, and see how far it rolls along the numbered floor. The box may get stuck on a low ramp.
- In, On, Under: Coco asks you to put a toy on the table, under it, in the box or next to it.
- More life in other games: the Busy Board knob keeps spinning when flicked, the fishing bobber swings on its line, sprinkles fall and bounce onto the cupcake, Mochi's ball arcs and bounces, and fruit drops into the basket with a squash.
- Super Coco! Coco's own superhero, in her red cape and blue mask. In the new Super Coco game (in Play) you fly her around to rescue a kitty from a tree, catch a runaway balloon, put an egg back in its nest and get the puppy's ball off the roof. She also has a new book in Stories: Super Coco Saves the Day.
- In, On, Under sits on a single floor, and the blocks in Big to Small are bigger on iPhone.
- Two new friends for Coco: Sprinkle the unicorn and Bolt the robot, each with a book in Stories (Coco and Sprinkle the Unicorn, Bolt Builds a Bridge).
- Coloring: 13 new pages in real coloring-book line art: Sprinkle, Bolt, Princess Marisol, a mermaid, a butterfly, a T-Rex, a rainbow castle, an ice cream cone, a kitty, an owl, a rocket, a race car and a fairy. That makes 21 pages.
- Coloring looks like real crayon now: a little paper shows through the color, and lines hidden behind something stay hidden.
- The home screen is simpler: three big choices, Learn, Play and Team Up. The science games are in Learn, the quiet toys (Busy Board, Blocks, Garden) are in Play, and Day & Night and Helper are in Team Up with the other things you do with a grown-up.
- Princess Marisol is drawn head to toe now, with lighter skin, in an orange gown, waving with a big smile, the same way Avery is drawn. The T-Rex in Dino Dig got shiny eyes and a toothy grin.
