- New game in Learn: Read to Coco. Read everyday phrases out loud to Coco, like "I see a cat." and "I love you.", sliding her little tab along under each word. A heart marks the common words to know by heart, dots under letters play their sounds, and tapping a word says it. The picture's shadow is a hint, and when you finish the picture appears and Bella reads it back.
- New book in Read: Coco's Dangerous Book, a silly story where the book won't behave, full of big fun words to read: POP! BUZZ! WHOOSH! BOOM! BOO! SPLASH! Slide a finger to a big word and it jumps and wobbles.
- Princesses! Coco's princess friends (Princess Marisol from the Dominican Republic, Luna, Pearl and Ruby) visit the Hair Salon in their crowns and Dress Up in their gowns, and star in a new book, Princess Marisol's Crown.
- Books: the beach pages show a real ocean wave instead of a waving hand.
